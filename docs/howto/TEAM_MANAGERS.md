# Team scheduler for team managers

- **Leave and swaps:** your associates' leave and swaps wait for you first, then the RTA. The count is on the menu.
- **Ask for coaching** (Requests page, or the RTA tab): pick the associates (use the search box), the day (today or a
  later day), billable or not, the length, and a time from the list. Each time shows the buffer for all of them together.
- Billable follows the program's setup: with interval compliance a billable session does not take people off the
  intervals and a non-billable one does; with service level or volume weighted both count. A warning shows when over 30%
  of the associates on shift would be off the queue, with the percentage.
- The RTA approves, rejects, or approves at a nearby time (with a comment) and may correct billable. You see the answer
  and comment as **New answer**, once. You can **Cancel** a request until it is decided. If you hold RTA rights you can
  approve your own request.

© 2026 Omar Mokhtar. All rights reserved.

`gen.py` writes the sample pages next to `ag.css`; `shots.py <out>` renders them in both looks. They need two folders
that are not kept here: `fonts/` (Archivo variable and Atkinson Hyperlegible Next, latin, from Google Fonts) and
`icons/` (Phosphor regular SVGs from the npm package `@phosphor-icons/core` 2.1.1, MIT). Made-up "Associate NN"
names; the week grid's colours are sample data.

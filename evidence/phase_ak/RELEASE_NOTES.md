# What is new in this package (Phase AK, 15 Oct 2026)

© 2026 Omar Mokhtar. All rights reserved.

For the RTA team, team managers and admins. Made from the Phase AK commits on top of the Phase AJ package.

## New

- **Team managers.** A new Team manager role next to Supervisor. On People, each associate has a Team manager pick,
  and "Set the team manager of several associates" gives many at once. Only an active team manager who shares one
  of the associate's programs can be picked.
- **Team managers approve their own team.** The first approval of an associate's leave or swap is their team
  manager's. An admin or a supervisor can approve in their place. An associate with no team manager can be approved
  by any team manager of the program. A swap between two teams needs both team managers; one approval is enough
  when both people share one.
- **Where each request waits.** The Requests page and My week say who a request is waiting for, for example
  "Waiting for Team Manager 01, the team manager of Associate 003" or "Approved by Team Manager 01, waiting for the
  RTA".
- **Roles.** Admins set what planners, team managers and supervisors may see and do, page by page and action by
  action (the RTA, schedules, runs, insights, exports, coaching, approvals, people and setup). The website checks
  these on every page and every change, not only in the menu. Admins keep everything, and Home always opens. The
  starting settings keep what planners and supervisors could do before; team managers start with seeing the pages
  and approving their team. Putting every role back to the starting settings asks for a tick first.
- **Times in on a schedule.** A schedule opened from the Schedules list can show its shifts in another time zone.
  Shifts are changed in the schedule's own zone; the page links back to it.

## Changed

- **Fix the day's breaks, Rescue the day and Change several breaks work on the 24 hours from "Day starts at"**, not
  from 00:00. A night shift that began the day before can be fixed too, and each change is kept on that shift's own
  day. One Undo takes the change back.
- **Channel colours on the Timeline.** Phone dark blue, Chat pink, Email light green, on a grey bar. Stretches of 30
  minutes or more show the letter P, C or E, so the channel can be read without the colour.

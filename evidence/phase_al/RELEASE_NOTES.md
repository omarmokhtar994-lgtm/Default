# What is new in this package (Phase AL, 15 Oct 2026)

© 2026 Omar Mokhtar. All rights reserved.

For the RTA team and planners. Made from the Phase AL commits on top of the Phase AK package.

## New

- **The engine's more consistent schedule is kept.** When a run finds a steadier week (fewer start-time changes,
  coverage no worse, passed by the independent check), it now appears beside the schedule the engine picked, as
  "Tool, more consistent". Its line on the Schedules list says how much steadier it is, for example "66 h to 53 h of
  start-time changes, 60 to 59 different starts". It is only offered: set it in use if you want it. Runs that
  finished before this package get it once when the website starts, while their files are still on the server.
- **Fairness on Plan channels.** A new Fairness section shows, for the week and then for the day picked, each
  person's time and share on Phone, Chat, Email and all channels. People are compared only with those who work the
  same channels and speak the same language. It shows how far each share is from even, names whoever is furthest
  from even (10 points or more), and the longest time on one channel in a day. Breaks are not counted as channel
  time. Coverage and the planner do not change.

## Changed

- **Email is deep green on the Timeline** (`#2F7D5B`), with a white E like Phone and Chat.

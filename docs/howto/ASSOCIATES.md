# Team scheduler for associates

You see two pages: **My day** and **My weeks**. Every time on them is in the schedule's own time; the page says which
zone that is.

## My day
- **Today** shows your shift and your breaks as they stand now.
- **Change** next to a break asks the RTA to move it. You can ask for **today** and **tomorrow**, not later days.
- Each time on the list shows a buffer: **High** (very likely approved), **Moderate**, or **Low** (likely rejected: nobody
  to spare in that interval). A warning shows when many people would be off the queue then.
- You may have a daily limit on requests, or requests may be switched off for your program. The page says so.
- After you send it, it shows as waiting. You can cancel it until the RTA decides. If nobody decides before the break's
  time, it expires and you are told.
- When the RTA decides, a **New answer** box shows the answer and any comment, once. If the RTA moved the time, it says
  "approved at 12:30 instead of 12:00". If someone else changes one of your breaks, **Your breaks changed** tells you.

## My weeks
- The week table lists each day's shift with its breaks. Use the week picker for later weeks.
- **Request leave** goes to your team manager first, then the RTA.
- **Offer a swap** (one day or the whole week) goes to your colleague, then both team managers. A rule that would be
  broken shows as a warning.
- Answers show here as **New answer**, once.

## Your password
- A temporary password works for 7 days. At first sign-in you choose your own; it must differ from the temporary one.
- Signed in on a lost phone? **Settings, Sign out other devices.**

# Phase BN samples: a luxury redesign (owner, 2026-11-12)

Three looks drawn over the real pages of the review server (made-up "Associate NN" data), by injecting one stylesheet each. Nothing on the site has
changed. The look names in the top bar still read Tide and Night watch because the samples ride on the existing looks.

- **A, Atelier (light):** porcelain page, white panels on layered shadows, deep ink, burnished brass marks what is picked.
- **B, Lacquer (dark):** blue-black lacquer, panels with a lit top edge and deep shadow, champagne marks what is picked.
- **C, Platinum (graphite):** camera-body grey, hairlines not shadows, condensed headings, no colour accent.

In all three: pages cross-fade into each other (CSS view transitions), buttons press in, fields and tabs ease; all off under reduced motion. Status
and channel colours are unchanged (colour still means state). Fonts are the two already self-hosted. These samples re-skin the pages; they do not
yet change layouts.

Files: `common.css`, `a_atelier.css`, `b_lacquer.css`, `c_platinum.css`, `shoot.py`, `shots/` (sign-in, home, RTA, week, overview at 1440 px; home and RTA at 390 px).

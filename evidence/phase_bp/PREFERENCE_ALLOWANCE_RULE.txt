Phase BP: shift Preferences may cost a little coverage (pre-registered rule, written before the code)
Owner, 2026-11-12: the asked shift is not given though people are available (a), and the "closer to preferences" version is missing or not in
use (c); a preference may cost coverage "not that much, maybe 1-2% maximum".

What was measured first (scratch runs, whole engine, 300 s, made-up agents)
- 12 people asking the shift demand needs anyway: 60 of 60 honoured. Requests in random order plus three one-day requests: 59 of 60; the one
  missed costs coverage. PREFERENCE_SWAP_POLISH made 0 swaps in both, so no PREFERENCE_CANDIDATE was published.
- Cause of (a) and (c): after Stage 1 the only preference step is a same-day swap between two people where neither ends further from the
  request and no guarded measure moves. One person cannot be moved to the asked shift alone, even when that costs nothing, and any cost is refused.

The change (engine/_tools/l632_universal_scheduler.py, preference_swap_polish; nothing else in the engine)
1. After the swaps, one associate's worked day may be MOVED to the asked shift when: the asked shift is a legal shift of the same length; the
   cell is not a Fixed request and the person is not nested; the row rules and the cell rules the swap already uses hold; employee quality is
   no worse; the pair stays in the same candidate class with no more hard failures. The break pattern stays with the person-day.
2. Free moves first: a move is kept when no guarded measure is worse (the swap's own guard).
3. Then moves inside the allowance, cheapest first. PREFERENCE_COVERAGE_ALLOWANCE = 0.02 (the owner's maximum). Against the SELECTED schedule,
   cumulatively over all kept moves:
   - every guarded higher-is-better coverage count (after/before target, floor, 100, 90, 80, week boundary) may be lower by at most
     floor(0.02 x the selected schedule's value);
   - every guarded lower-is-better measure (gaps, zero-staffed quarters, language and opening gaps, break concurrency, split gaps, violations)
     stays no worse, except the overage measures (names containing "overage"): a moved person is surplus where they land, which is the cost
     the owner accepted, and shortage is already bounded by the line above.
4. The selected schedule is still never rewritten (F-35). The result is published as PREFERENCE_CANDIDATE with its cost in the audit record
   (moves, allowance, each measure that fell and by how much). The runner offers it when the independent validator's numbers are inside the
   same allowance, and its text says what it costs instead of "same coverage".
5. Use Preferences = No turns the whole step off, as before. allowance = 0 gives the old strict behaviour plus free moves.

Acceptance (A/B: engine before the change against after, same seed 9000, 2 workers, same time limit)
- Shipped workbooks (no shift Preferences in any): selected schedule and every published file identical in content; no PREFERENCE_CANDIDATE.
- Protected RC9.1 baseline workbooks and engine/regression_assets: untouched bytes.
- Toy with one-day requests (59 of 60 today): the candidate honours more requests than the selected schedule, every higher-is-better count is
  within floor(2%) of the selected schedule's, no lower-is-better non-overage measure is worse, independent validation PASS.
- Toy where everything is honoured already: no candidate, selected schedule identical.
- Gate: run_tests.sh green with the minimum raised by the new tests.
If any line fails, the change is not shipped.

Not decided here (the owner's): whether the website should put the preference version in use by itself. It stays offered, one click to use.

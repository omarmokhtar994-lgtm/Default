Where the group link comes from (about two minutes, once per group)
  1. Teams: in the channel, More options (…) > Workflows > "Send webhook alerts to a channel". Name it "Team Scheduler", pick the team and channel, then copy the link it gives you. The workflow belongs to whoever makes it, so a shared account is safer.
  2. Slack: api.slack.com/apps > Create New App > From scratch > Incoming Webhooks > On > Add New Webhook > pick the channel, then copy the link.
  3. Paste the link here and Save, then Send test message. Anyone holding the link can post to the group, so paste it only here.

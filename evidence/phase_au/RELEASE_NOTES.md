# Phase AU release notes (with AP to AT in the same package)

- Break change and coaching requests: decided on the RTA tab; for today and tomorrow; overlap, stale and expiry checks;
  new requests posted to the group; per-program on/off, lead time and daily limit.
- Associates: Today and My weeks pages; answers to break, coaching, leave and swap requests in the site and the group;
  told when their breaks are changed; the zone their times are in.
- Group posts: only the latest change to a break, with the person's breaks as they now stand.
- Break shape in Plan breaks automatically (first break 2 to 3.5 h, lunch 2 to 3 h later, last before the last hour).
- People: download the list, switch several off or on, sign out other devices, sign-in history, held addresses.
- Security: temporary passwords expire in 7 days and cannot be kept; sign-in forgives what a paste adds; a zip bomb is
  refused; an address is held after 40 wrong sign-ins; housekeeping for renamed LOBs and people who leave.
- Accessibility: impact tables scroll on a phone; the day look's `--over-text` darkened for contrast (4.37 to over 4.5).
Docs: `docs/howto/`.

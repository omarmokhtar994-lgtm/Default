One pattern on every page

  • Top bar: Go to (Ctrl K) search, the look, then one account menu holding Settings, Change password and Sign out.
  • Title row: page title on the left; the one main action on the right (New schedule, Add a person, Save).
  • Tabs: one row under the title, same place on every page. RTA keeps its seven; Volumes keeps its six; People, Weeks and Schedules get tabs in place of one long page (People: Accounts, Add, Bulk, Team managers, Sign-in holds; Weeks: Achieved, Hours needed, Overtime, Extra hours; Schedules: Versions, Shifts, Channels, Checks, Changes).
  • Other actions: one quiet row under the tabs (downloads, split view, handover). Delete and other risky actions sit last, alone.
  • Where am I: Back and Home stay; the menu marks the page; Go to finds any page, person or week.

Why these moves

  • Insights held three different jobs. Planning (Volumes) joins Plan; Exports stays with Reports; Team joins People.
  • Coaching requests exist but had no menu entry.
  • Setup mixed people with program settings; admin-only pages get their own group, and Programs joins LOBs and defaults.

# What is new in this package: Phase AS (break changes and coaching requests)

© 2026 Omar Mokhtar. All rights reserved.

## Associates

- **My breaks** (from My week): today's breaks, and for each break that has not started "Change". It lists the times the
  break may move to (inside the shift, past 00:00 for a night shift, within the program's gaps between breaks), each
  with its buffer: High and Moderate are most likely approved, Low says it is likely to be rejected because there is no
  spare cover. The request goes to the RTA and can be cancelled until it is decided. Only the running day.

## Team managers

- **Ask for coaching** (Requests page): pick one or more associates, the day (today or later), a length, a time and the
  department. Each time shows the buffer for all of them together. The request goes to the RTA. A person with RTA rights
  approves their own request.

## RTA

- **Break changes and coaching** on the Requests page: each waiting request with the intervals it touches, the people
  needed, on the floor now and after the change, and the buffer after. Approve makes the break move or the coaching on the
  day, with every check the RTA's own forms run (one person who cannot be coached stops the whole request); Reject needs a
  reason the requester reads. The count of waiting requests includes them.

## Buffer words

High: at least one person above the need in the tightest interval the move touches, once the person is off the queue.
Moderate: at the need. Low: below it. (Owner's answer, 21 Oct 2026.)
